RNG Collector

Live a whole life. Collect 💰 and ❤️, and dodge everything that falls.

▶ Play Game ♾️ Play Endless

Play Game: reach age 60 to unlock Rebirth. You keep your relics and get permanent bonuses.

Endless: no rebirth, and the dangers ramp up faster. How old can you get?

Every 10 years the game pauses and you make a life choice. Controls: A/D or arrows to move, W / Up / Space to jump.
